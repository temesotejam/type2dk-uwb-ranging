# Third-party software

These BIN files are firmware for the Murata Type2DK evaluation board containing NXP QN9090 and SR040 devices. They incorporate components from `UWBIOT_SR040_v04.03.14_MCUx` and the Type2DK board adaptation supplied by Murata.

The NXP SDK, vendor patches, vendor libraries, and toolchain are separate dependencies; their source archives are not redistributed in this repository. The application sources, build scripts, and tests here do not grant rights to those dependencies. No blanket open-source license is asserted for the complete firmware.

The vendor license and software content register accompanying the SDK are retained in [licenses/EULA.pdf](licenses/EULA.pdf) and [licenses/SCR.txt](licenses/SCR.txt). The content register identifies the NXP proprietary, BSD, Apache, and MIT components. FreeRTOS's supplied license is retained in [licenses/FreeRTOS-LICENSE.md](licenses/FreeRTOS-LICENSE.md). Additional notices from the compiled SDK source files are retained in [licenses/SDK-COPYRIGHT-NOTICES.txt](licenses/SDK-COPYRIGHT-NOTICES.txt).

Use the firmware and dependencies subject to their applicable terms and for the supported NXP-based hardware. The SDK ZIP and Murata ZIP must be obtained through an authorized distribution channel.
